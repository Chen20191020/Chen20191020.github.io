Research

I study modeling and inference for dependent data, with an emphasis on empirical performance and interpretability.

My work develops structural representations of locally stationary time series, statistical methods for classification, and simultaneous inference for time-varying dependence. I am interested in rigorous statistical guarantees and applications to complex multichannel signals.

Research Projects

  • Structural Classification of Locally Stationary Time Series

    Joint work with Xiucai Ding and Lexin Li

    Nov. 2024 – Apr. 2026
    • Developed a classifier using time-varying autoregressive models to extract interpretable features of temporal dependence.
    • Established feature consistency and asymptotic classification guarantees under class-separation conditions.
    • Evaluated normal/abnormal EEG classification on the TUH Abnormal EEG Corpus, obtaining 94.7% accuracy on 150 test subjects.

  • Structural Approximation of Multivariate Time Series and Multichannel EEG Classification

    Joint work with Xiucai Ding and Jeffrey Wang

    Jan. 2026 – Present
    • Implemented estimation, cross-validated model selection, and classification algorithms; conducted simulations across dependence patterns and class-balance settings.
    • Analyzed the Siena Scalp EEG Database for seizure/non-seizure classification, correctly identifying 61 of 64 test events (95.3% accuracy; F1 score 0.914).

  • Constrained Autocorrelation Estimation and Simultaneous Inference

    Joint work with Xiucai Ding and Zhou Zhou

    June 2026 – Present
    • Formulated time-varying autocorrelation estimation using constrained sieve regression and constructed simultaneous confidence bands.
    • Implemented a block multiplier procedure and evaluated confidence-band coverage in simulations; developing proofs of uniform consistency and asymptotic validity.
