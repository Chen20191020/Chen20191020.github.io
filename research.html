Research

My research focuses on statistical learning, inference, and reliability for dependent data, particularly nonstationary time series, with an emphasis on real-world applications.

My future research aims to develop mathematically principled, interpretable, and reliable methods for dependent and sequential data. I am interested in further developing and adapting time series classification methodology to more complex real-world problems, foundation models for health applications with few labeled subjects, and statistical reliability for dependent and nonstationary data.

Research Experience

  • Structural Classification of Locally Stationary Time Series

    Joint work with Xiucai Ding and Lexin Li; JRSS Series B, 2026

    Nov. 2024 – Apr. 2026
    • Developed a complete classification procedure for locally stationary time series, leveraging the autoregressive approximation framework for nonstationary time series to extract interpretable features of temporal dependence.
    • Designed and conducted simulation studies to evaluate parameter selection and robustness across different scenarios; performed real-data classification on the TUH Abnormal EEG Corpus, achieving 94.7% accuracy on 150 test subjects using the Cz channel.
    • Collaborated with my advisor to establish feature consistency and asymptotic classification guarantees under class-separation conditions.

  • Structural Approximation of Multivariate Time Series and Multichannel EEG Classification

    Joint work with Xiucai Ding and Jeffrey Wang; completed manuscript

    Jan. 2026 – Present
    • Implemented the classification procedure and developed strategies for dimension selection, norm selection, and hyperparameter tuning; conducted simulation studies across various dependence structures and class-balance settings.
    • Conducted comparisons with existing modern classification methods in both simulations and real-data applications.
    • Performed end-to-end multichannel EEG analysis on the Siena Scalp EEG Database, including preprocessing, parameter tuning, channel and norm selection, achieving 95.3% classification accuracy (61 of 64 test events) and an F1 score of 0.914.

  • Constrained Autocorrelation Estimation and Simultaneous Inference

    Joint work with Xiucai Ding and Zhou Zhou; work in progress

    June 2026 – Present
    • Developed the constrained estimator for the time-varying autocorrelation function of locally stationary time series and constructed simultaneous confidence bands using the multiplier bootstrap procedure.
    • Designed and conducted simulation studies to evaluate estimation accuracy and confidence-band coverage.
    • Currently establishing uniform consistency of the estimator and asymptotic validity of the simultaneous confidence bands.
