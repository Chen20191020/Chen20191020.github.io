About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of California, Davis, advised by Professor Xiucai Ding. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in June 2027.

My research focuses on time series, statistical learning, and inference for dependent data. I develop methods for time series classification and time-varying dependence estimation, combining theoretical analysis with empirical evaluation. I am particularly interested in interpretable statistical methods that can address problems with real-world impact.

Previously, I received an M.S. in Statistics from UC Davis and a B.S. in Applied Statistics from the Complutense University of Madrid.

Research Interests

  • Locally stationary time series
  • Classification of dependent data
  • Time-varying dependence estimation
  • Statistical inference and uncertainty quantification
