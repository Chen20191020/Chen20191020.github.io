About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of California, Davis (expected June 2027), advised by Prof. Xiucai Ding, Department of Statistics, UC Davis.

My research focuses on statistical learning, inference, and reliability for dependent data, particularly nonstationary time series, with an emphasis on real-world applications.

My future research aims to develop mathematically principled, interpretable, and reliable methods for dependent and sequential data.

Research Interests

  • Statistical Learning & Inference
  • Statistical Reliability

  • EEG, BCI & Neural Speech Decoding
