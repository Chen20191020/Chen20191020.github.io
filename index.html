About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of California, Davis. Before starting my Ph.D., I earned an M.S. in Statistics from UC Davis and a B.S. in Applied Statistics from the Complutense University of Madrid.

My research focuses on statistical and deep learning methods for sequential data, with particular emphasis on nonstationary signals and temporal dependence modeling. My goal is to develop interpretable, scalable, and data-driven methods that can extract meaningful patterns from complex temporal data and support real-world decision making.

I am currently seeking Summer 2027 internship opportunities, particularly in roles such as Applied Scientist, Research Scientist, Machine Learning Scientist, Data Scientist. I am especially interested in opportunities involving sequential data, user behavior analytics, biomedical signals, and other temporal datasets.

Outside of my academic work, I enjoy soccer, table tennis, and traveling. You can contact me at mrcqian@ucdavis.edu.

Research Interests

  • Nonstationary time series analysis
  • Statistical inference under temporal dependence
  • Deep learning for sequential data
  • Time-frequency feature extraction

Current Research

I am developing estimation and simultaneous inference methods for locally stationary time series. This work combines sieve-based estimation, convergence analysis, and multiplier bootstrap methods to construct simultaneous confidence bands.
