About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of California, Davis. Before starting my Ph.D., I earned an M.S. in Statistics from UC Davis and a B.S. in Applied Statistics from the Complutense University of Madrid.

My research interests include nonparametric time series, stochastic processes, asymptotic theory, and statistical inference for dependent data. I am particularly interested in locally stationary processes, where the underlying temporal structure evolves over time.

Outside of my academic work, I enjoy football and traveling. You can contact me at mrcqian@ucdavis.edu.

Research Interests

  • Nonparametric time series
  • Locally stationary processes
  • Asymptotic theory and uniform convergence
  • Bootstrap and simultaneous inference

Current Research

I am developing simultaneous inference methods for time-varying dependence in locally stationary time series. This work combines orthonormal-basis sieve estimation, convergence analysis under polynomially decaying physical dependence, and Gaussian multiplier bootstrap methods for constructing simultaneous confidence bands.
