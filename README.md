# Chen Qian — Academic Website

A minimal academic website, hosted with GitHub Pages and styled with restrained slate-blue accents.

## Pages

- `index.html` — about, research interests, selected publication, and education
- `research.html` — publications (published, submitted, and work in progress) and research experience
- `experience.html` — honors, awards, and teaching experience
- `software.html` — Sie2nts and SIMle R packages, with DOI, CRAN and user-guide links
- `assets/Chen_Qian_CV.pdf` — exact latest 3-page CV uploaded October 21, 2026; the CV navigation link points directly to this PDF
- `cv.html` — compatibility redirect to the latest PDF for existing links

## Sources

Professional and publication details follow the supplied October 21, 2026 academic CV. The About Me and Research Interests wording is based on the supplied BCBI research statement; future interests are identified as aims, not completed projects. Material absent from the updated CV is omitted.

## Design and deployment

The existing minimalist layout, typography, photograph, and restrained color palette are retained. Styles live in `assets/styles.css`; publishing uses the `main` branch.
